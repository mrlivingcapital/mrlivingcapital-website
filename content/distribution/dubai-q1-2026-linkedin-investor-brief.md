# LinkedIn investor brief — Dubai Q1 2026

**Dubai recorded 43,200 property transactions in Q1 2026. Volume-driven growth is not the same as price-driven growth.**

My review of the quarter’s DLD figures shows:

- **Transactions:** 43,200, up **11.1%** year on year.
- **Total value:** **AED 122.4 billion**, up **12.6%**.
- **Average transaction value:** up only **1.4%**.
- **Off-plan share:** **62%**, compared with **38%** for the secondary market.

More transactions are taking place at roughly similar price points. That is not evidence of a uniform luxury boom.

The corridor differences matter more:

- **Downtown:** stronger appreciation, weaker yield characteristics.
- **Business Bay and JVC:** lower appreciation, with income and supply analysis taking priority.
- **Dubai South:** long-term infrastructure potential that still depends on delivery and absorption.

Then comes the supply test: **78,000 units were scheduled for delivery during 2026**, with a significant share concentrated in Q3 and Q4. Delivery timing and genuine tenant demand will determine how individual corridors absorb that supply.

**My bottom line:** this is a selective market, not a simple boom or bust. Corridor choice, entry basis, developer delivery record and exit audience matter more than the headline growth rate.

If you are deploying capital, start with your objective, timeline, currency exposure and downside—not a generic “best project” list.
