P02/08

Market function

Use Ras Al Khaimah when the allocation deliberately accepts a developing-market risk profile in exchange for tourism exposure and a potential income case. Underwrite current demand before assigning value to future catalysts.

راس‌الخیمه مسیر رشد گردشگری است با نیاز به پذیرش خطر بیشتر.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P03/08

Demand drivers

Test hospitality, local employment, second-home and long-stay demand separately. A tourism narrative does not establish year-round residential occupancy, so each demand source requires its own evidence.

تقاضای گردشگری، اشتغال و اقامت بلندمدت باید جداگانه سنجیده شود.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P04/08

Connectivity

Assess access to employment, hospitality and community services using current journeys. Future transport or tourism infrastructure belongs in an upside scenario until delivery is verified.

دسترسی فعلی از زیرساخت برنامه‌ریزی‌شده آینده مهم‌تر است.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P05/08

Supply

Map competing waterfront, hospitality-branded and residential stock by delivery date and unit type. New supply can weaken both rent and resale leverage even when the visitor narrative is strengthening.

عرضه آینده می‌تواند بر اجاره و قدرت فروش مجدد فشار بیاورد.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P06/08

Risks

The principal risks are thinner exit liquidity, unproven tenant depth, catalyst delay and yield assumptions that omit fees or vacancy. Model a downside case without the expected tourism uplift.

نقدشوندگی محدود و تأخیر در محرک‌های رشد خطرهای اصلی هستند.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P07/08

Investor fit

Best aligned with risk-tolerant, longer-horizon investors who can tolerate a less certain exit. Less aligned with capital that may need rapid resale or predictable near-term occupancy.

این مسیر برای سرمایه‌گذار بلندمدت با تحمل خطر مناسب‌تر است.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P08/08

Verdict

Ras Al Khaimah can serve as a measured tourism and yield allocation, not a guaranteed growth shortcut. Verify net income, supply and exit liquidity before the catalyst case. This is general market intelligence, not personalised financial advice.

راس‌الخیمه می‌تواند نقش درآمد و گردشگری داشته باشد؛ خروج باید سنجیده شود.
