The corridor

The area agents laughed at two years ago now posts Dubai's highest weekly transaction counts. The logic is infrastructure: the Al Maktoum International Airport expansion — set to become the world's largest — plus Expo City's permanent district. Entry prices sit well below the city median of AED 1,710 psf, which is exactly why both end users and yield investors are positioning early.

Who it suits

Capital-growth investors with a 5–10 year horizon; first-time buyers priced out of the inner corridors; logistics-linked commercial plays around the airport zone.

The off-plan angle

Off-plan dominates here — most of the district is still being built. In Q2's low-launch environment, credible Dubai South releases with airport-anchored timelines are the corridor's sweet spot. Early is uncomfortable; late is expensive.
