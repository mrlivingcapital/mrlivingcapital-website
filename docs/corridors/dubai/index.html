P02/08

Market function

Use Dubai when the allocation requires a deep buyer pool, established transaction infrastructure and a comparatively clear exit audience. Judge each asset by verified activity rather than the strength of the city narrative.

نقش دبی در سبد سرمایه‌گذاری، نقدشوندگی و دسترسی به خریداران بین‌المللی است.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P03/08

Demand drivers

Test the depth and diversity of real demand for the specific district and unit. Separate end-user needs from investor momentum and identify who is likely to rent or buy the asset after the current owner.

تقاضای واقعی هر منطقه و هر واحد باید جدا از هیجان بازار سنجیده شود.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P04/08

Connectivity

Measure operational access, daily journey times and delivered infrastructure. Treat announced connections as future scenarios until their funding, construction and opening are independently verified.

دسترسی عملیاتی و زمان واقعی سفر از وعده‌های آینده مهم‌تر است.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P05/08

Supply

Compare competing ready stock with realistic future completions at district and unit-type level. A liquid city can still contain an oversupplied micro-market, so entry price must reflect the alternatives available to the next buyer.

عرضه باید در سطح منطقه و نوع واحد سنجیده شود، نه فقط در سطح شهر.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P06/08

Risks

The principal risks are paying a liquidity premium without receiving liquidity at asset level, overestimating net income and underestimating competing supply. Pressure-test service costs, resale time and the exit buyer pool.

هزینه‌های خدمات، مدت فروش مجدد و عمق تقاضا باید با دقت ارزیابی شوند.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P07/08

Investor fit

Best aligned with investors who value exit optionality, a broad buyer audience and an established market process. Less aligned with strategies whose return case depends only on a high headline yield.

این مسیر برای سرمایه‌گذاری مناسب است که گزینه خروج و عمق بازار را در اولویت می‌گذارد.

Source: MR Living Capital, Three Emirates Comparison, 20 May 2026; qualitative editorial thesis · As of 2026-05-20

P08/08

Verdict

Dubai can serve as the liquidity component of a UAE allocation. The city thesis is not sufficient: the district, unit, net economics and realistic exit must each survive independent review. This is general market intelligence, not personalised financial advice.

دبی می‌تواند جزء نقدشونده یک سبد باشد؛ تصمیم نهایی به واحد و خروج واقعی بستگی دارد.
